2

Senior Materials Scientist

Gracefield Innovation Quarter, Wellington (On-site) · Permanent, Full-time

About the role

Xplora is a deep-tech startup developing next-generation heat storage for sustainable energy applications. Our vision is to move energy beyond boundaries — of time and place. We are seeking a motivated Senior Materials Scientist to support the development, optimisation, testing, and scale-up of advanced functional materials.

This role provides hands-on experience working on real scientific challenges, from material formulation and performance evaluation to scale-up and technology development, while contributing to commercially relevant R&D.

If you're curious, hands-on, and hungry to grow, this might be the role you've been waiting for. We value people who are:

  • Humble — open to learning, self-aware, and collaborative
  • Hungry — driven to build, explore, and get stuff done
  • Smart — emotionally and interpersonally intelligent

What you'll do

At Xplora, we are developing a novel composite material for use in thermochemical energy storage — a system that uses reversible chemical reactions to store heat and release it on demand, with minimal loss over time. Your mission is to help design, validate, optimise and scale up this material so it:

  • Achieves high energy density
  • Maintains stable performance over multiple charge/discharge cycles
  • Operates within target regeneration temperatures and heat output
  • Retains robust structural integrity during repeated cycling

You'll also play a key role in translating material discoveries into manufacturable components for reactor integration.

Ideal candidate

  • Master's or PhD degree in Materials Science, Chemistry, or a related discipline
  • 3+ years working experience in a material-related field
  • Experience in porous materials, adsorption technologies, thermochemical reactions, gas-solid systems, and sustainable energy applications
  • Hands-on experience with material formulation, processing, and performance evaluation
  • Familiarity with structure–property relationships in porous materials, including sorption/desorption behaviour, material stability, and heat/mass transfer
  • Understanding of reaction kinetics and thermodynamics, especially for endothermic/exothermic cycling processes
  • Proficiency with characterisation techniques such as gas sorption analysis, PXRD, DSC/TGA, SEM, and microscopy is advantageous
  • Experience with pellet production, reactor testing, process scale-up, numerical modelling, or experimental system development is beneficial

Why join us

At Xplora, you won't just observe innovation — you'll be part of building it. We are tackling complex energy challenges through the development of next-generation materials and technologies, where every experiment, insight, and improvement contributes to a larger mission. As part of our team, you will gain hands-on experience working on meaningful R&D projects, collaborate with scientists and engineers, and see how ideas move from the laboratory towards real-world applications.

We value courage, ownership, and growth. We are looking for people who enjoy solving challenging problems, are eager to learn, and want to contribute to building technology that can make a lasting impact.

If this excites you, we'd love to hear from you!
